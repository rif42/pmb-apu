Why Join the Pre-University Program at APU?
A structured pathway to Agung Putra University. Combining an international curriculum with project-based learning.
  • The Pre-University Program at APU bridges the transition from high school to university life.
  • Students develop intellectual, emotional, and mental readiness to adapt to a global-standard academic environment.
  • Students can select courses based on their interests in Science, Health, or Social & Humanities.
  • Participants receive official certificates that may be recognized as academic credits (SKS) upon admission to Agung Putra University.
  • The curriculum follows 60% international standards and integrates Artificial Intelligence (AI) to support modern learning.
  • Pre-University students gain access to a dedicated admission pathway to APU’s undergraduate programs, without going through highly competitive general entrance exams.
  • Students have the opportunity to apply for the APU Scholarship Program.

Project-based learning outcomes can be used as academic portfolios for university applications, both locally and internationally.

  • Learning is delivered through a combination of online and offline classes, allowing students to study flexibly from anywhere.
  • Students are guided by experienced Agung Putra University lecturers and supported by high-quality digital learning materials.
